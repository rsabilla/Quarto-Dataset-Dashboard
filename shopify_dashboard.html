About This Dashboard

Dataset: Shopify Sales Dataset for ML & EDA Source: Kaggle — aliiihussain Description: A realistic e-commerce dataset containing Shopify order-level data for sales analysis and customer behavior insights.

Story

This dashboard tells the story of where revenue comes from, when customers buy, and which products drive the most value for a Shopify-based e-commerce store. Even someone unfamiliar with e-commerce can use these four pages to answer the most important business questions:

  • Overview — How is the store performing overall? What are the peak hours and order fulfillment health?
  • Products — Which products and categories generate the most revenue? Are there slow days that need promotion?
  • Geography — Where in the world are customers buying from? Which cities are highest-value?
  • About — Context and data source.
Key Takeaways
  1. A small number of product categories typically drive the majority of revenue (Pareto effect).
  2. Order volume peaks at specific hours of the day, suggesting optimal times for email campaigns.
  3. Certain days of the week consistently outperform others — useful for scheduling promotions.
  4. Geographic concentration reveals where marketing spend is most effective.
How to Use

Download the CSV from Kaggle, place it in the same folder as this .qmd file, and render with:

quarto render shopify_dashboard.qmd

If column names in your CSV differ from the defaults, update the mapping section at the top of the setup chunk.
